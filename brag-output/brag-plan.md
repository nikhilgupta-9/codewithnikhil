## 1. Creative Planning Rubric

1. **What is it?**  
   An elite full-stack web engineering & AI workflow automation platform that builds ultra-fast, high-converting websites (WordPress, Dynamic, MERN, eCommerce) and autonomous AI bots in days instead of months.

2. **Who is it for?**  
   Startups, B2B companies, eCommerce businesses, and local enterprises in Delhi NCR, Pan-India, and Global markets (USA, UK, UAE).

3. **What sets it apart?**  
   Zero agency bloat, 100/100 Google Lighthouse speed, native AI workflow integration (n8n, Gemini API, WhatsApp AI bots), and transparent fixed pricing with free interactive calculators.

4. **What is the most impressive claim?**  
   "From concept to live production in 3-7 days with 100/100 Core Web Vitals and built-in AI automated lead scoring."

5. **What is the visual hook?**  
   A high-energy dark cyber grid with electric lime pulsing nodes, live speed tests hitting 100/100, and AI workflows connecting in real-time.

6. **What real UI/flow is shown?**  
   - Real Hero Command Center with Quick Utilities
   - Live Website Cost Calculator & SEO Auditor
   - AI Workflow node architecture diagram
   - Instant 1-Click WhatsApp & Online Pay system

7. **Tone**:  
   `polished` meets `cinematic` tech launch — confident, clean, high-impact typography, snappy motion.

8. **Duration**:  
   18 seconds total (6 sharp scenes).

9. **One-line Share Caption**:  
   "Stop waiting months for slow agency websites. Built with modern full-stack code & AI automations that actually convert."
